## Abstract

This research component develops an AI-based electricity demand behaviour intelligence system. It analyses 15-minute electricity demand data to identify, classify and predict daily demand behaviour.

- **Features:** each day becomes a 96-slot daily load profile.
- **Clustering:** K-Means and DBSCAN discover and validate the demand regimes in the data.
- **Anomaly detection:** Isolation Forest flags unusual days.
- **Labels:** a priority-ordered rule engine assigns each day one of four labels: **Normal Demand Day**, **Peak Demand Day**, **Abnormal Demand Day** or **Low Demand Day**.

The results are served through a REST API and an interactive React dashboard. The dashboard shows label and risk distributions, anomaly scores, the meaning of each cluster, model comparisons, and a seasonal-matching prediction for future dates.

Explanations of individual abnormal days come from the separate **Explainable AI (XAI) component** (LIME), which the dashboard opens with the "LIME →" button. LIME is not part of this component.

---

## Research Problem & Motivation

Planners need to know what kind of day each day is, which days are unusual, and what to expect on a future date. This component combines clustering, anomaly detection and rule-based classification into one pipeline that:

- Learns the main daily load patterns without labelled training data (clustering)
- Detects abnormal demand days (Isolation Forest, compared against LOF, One-Class SVM and z-scores)
- Gives every label a human-readable reason (rule-based labelling)
- Predicts the likely behaviour of a future date from historical patterns

---

## Research Objectives

1. Build 96-slot daily demand profiles from 15-minute interval electricity data
2. Cluster the daily profiles (K-Means, DBSCAN; compared with Ward and GMM) to discover the demand regimes
3. Detect anomalous demand days with Isolation Forest, selected by bootstrap stability against LOF, One-Class SVM and z-scores
4. Assign an interpretable behaviour label and risk level to every day with a priority-ordered rule engine
5. Predict future behaviour by seasonal matching on the labelled history
6. Deliver the results through a REST API and an interactive dashboard linked to the XAI component

## ML Pipeline — Detailed Steps

| Stage | Module | Method | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `data_loader.py`, `preprocessing.py`, `daily_profile.py` | Cleaning + pivot | Drop NaN/negative values and values above median + 10×IQR; interpolate gaps; drop readings outside Q1−3×IQR … Q3+3×IQR; resample to 15 min; pivot to 96-slot daily profiles (complete days only) with daily peak, mean and std |
| 2 | `clustering.py` | K-Means + DBSCAN | StandardScaler per slot; K-Means k chosen by silhouette over 2–12 (k = 2; Davies–Bouldin and Calinski–Harabasz agree); DBSCAN eps from the k-distance knee (min_samples = 8) |
| 3 | `anomaly_detection.py` | Isolation Forest | 300 trees, contamination = 3%; `final_is_anomaly` = Isolation Forest only. Z-scores of daily peak and mean are kept as diagnostic columns (they never reach 3.0 on this data) |
| 4 | `peak_detection.py` | Percentile thresholds | Peak day if peak ≥ 90th percentile, OR signed peak z-score > 2.0, OR (anomaly AND peak ≥ 75th percentile) |
| 5 | `behavior_labeling.py` | Priority-rule engine | Ordered rules assign the final label and risk level |

## Behaviour Classification System

Each day receives exactly one label from a **priority-ordered rule engine**:

| Priority | Label | Risk Level | Trigger Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (Highest) | **Abnormal Demand Day** | High | Anomaly flag = True AND peak flag = False |
| 2 | **Peak Demand Day** | Medium | Peak ≥ 90th percentile OR signed peak z-score > 2.0 OR (anomaly + peak ≥ 75th percentile) |
| 3 | **Low Demand Day** | Low | Mean daily demand < 25th percentile |
| 4 (Default) | **Normal Demand Day** | Normal | All other days |

**Current results (1,977 days):**

| Label | Days |
|---|---|
| Normal Demand Day | 1,279 |
| Low Demand Day | 465 |
| Peak Demand Day | 212 |
| Abnormal Demand Day | 21 |

- **Anomalies:** Isolation Forest flags 60 days (3.03%). 21 of them are labelled Abnormal; the other 39 have high peaks, so they are labelled Peak.
- **Clusters:** K-Means finds two regimes: cluster 0 = weekday pattern (1,396 days) and cluster 1 = weekend / low pattern (581 days).
- **Role of clustering:** it is used to discover and validate the demand regimes; the labels are rule-based for interpretability.

## Future Behaviour Prediction

The prediction module (`behavior_prediction.py`) predicts the label for any target date by seasonal matching:

1. **Primary match:** same month + same day-of-week in the labelled history
2. **Fallback 1:** same month only
3. **Fallback 2:** full history

The most frequent label among the matched days is returned. Its raw share (e.g. 22 of 22 matched days = 100%) is a frequency, not a calibrated confidence, and overstates certainty when few days match. The dashboard therefore shows a **likelihood from history** with Laplace (add-one) smoothing over the four labels: (count + 1) / (matched days + 4), so 22 of 22 becomes 88.5%. The response contains both the raw counts (`label_counts`, `raw_probability_distribution`, `raw_frequency_percent`) and the smoothed values (`probability_distribution`, `likelihood_percent`).

Demo dates with mixed history: **2027-07-03** (Saturday in July: Low 11, Normal 10, Peak 2), **2027-05-02** (Sunday in May: Normal 13, Low 12, Peak 1, Abnormal 1) and **2027-06-07** (Monday in June: Peak 11, Normal 10, predicts Peak).
